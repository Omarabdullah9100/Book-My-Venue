- Bundled vendor booking
- 360° tours
- Smart pricing suggestions (host panel)
- Admin demand-gap analytics beyond the basics

- Malayalam translation (all copy is in `src/lib/strings.ts`)
- Real bank payouts (the MVP keeps a payout ledger only)
- Mobile app

Out of scope entirely: institutional bookings and approval workflows.
